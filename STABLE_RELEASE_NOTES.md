Blocksy is a multiplayer block-building game for the web and the Nintendo 3DS (homebrew), with shared worlds, chat and friends.

## What's new since 1.4.0

### Changes
- Italian, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Chinese and Korean fully translated; every web language is complete.
- Visit counts on game cards (web and 3DS) and a limited tag on season rewards.
- 3DS menu click and notification sounds; Discover can be sorted by most visited.
- Meetups can be added to your calendar, and reminders respect Do not disturb.
- Home shows your next meetup.
- Health bar in Arena and Teams, and coin sounds on the 3DS shop.
- Home shows your season level.
- 3DS updater rewritten: verified downloads (SHA-256), resume after network loss, rollback for .3dsx, changes list and progress with speed.
- New 3DS: full speed mode and a longer draw distance.
- Studio parts (blocks, balls, cylinders, spawn points) are drawn and collide on the 3DS.
- New templates: Memory, Zones, Treasure hunt, Tower, Maze, Reflexes, Crumbling floor and Quiz.
- Create game: template filters and search.
- Six new achievements.
- Studio: castle, dome, spiral staircase and pillar structures.
- 3DS: instant game list on startup (cached on the SD card).
- 3DS: double jump, speed, gravity and spawn settings of games.
- 3DS: Show FPS setting; the game's welcome message appears on entry.
- 3DS: skies from the game setting (dawn, sunset, night, space).
- 3DS: Home shows your clan, season level and the next meetup; distant hills on the horizon and flowers on grass worlds.
- 3DS updater: test download without installing, free RAM and SD shown, adaptive draw quality.
- 3DS: translucent and glowing Studio parts; setting to turn off automatic stable installs.
- The 3DS pauses music during an update to free memory.
- Kill feed in Arena and Teams.
- Four new achievements for clans, meetups, collaboration and animations.
- New King of the hill multiplayer template.
- New Dodge multiplayer template.
- Clan chat messages can be reported and deleted by staff (needs supabase/auth/v19.sql).
- Moderation lists the currently suspended accounts (needs supabase/auth/v19.sql).
- Studio shows a notice when someone joins the co-editing session.
- Dodge gets harder with time.
- Palette shortcuts for Clan (g c) and Meetups (g e).
- Meetups can be reported and cancelled by staff (needs supabase/auth/v19.sql).
- Notification sound for meetup reminders and a fanfare when opening crates on the 3DS.
- API reference documents Tool objects, animations and RemoteEvent.
- New Lua snippets: Tool and part animation.
- Lap race shows your best time.
- Dodge celebrates every 10 seconds survived.

## Installing
- Install `Blocksy.cia` with FBI, or copy `Blocksy.3dsx` to the `3ds` folder of the SD card and run it from the Homebrew Launcher.
