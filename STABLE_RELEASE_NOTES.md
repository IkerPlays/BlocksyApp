Blocksy is a multiplayer block-building game for the web and the Nintendo 3DS (homebrew), with shared worlds, chat and friends.

## What's new since 1.4.1

### Changes
- Studio checklist with 3DS compatibility checks; fixed four untranslated texts.
- 3DS: friends show the game they are playing (X opens it); chat speech bubbles above players.
- 3DS: faces, backs, pets and trails in the Shop; pets and trails are drawn.
- 3DS: claim season levels and weekly missions from the Shop.
- 3DS: tree canopies in Hills and Islands are solid like on the web.
- 3DS: block debris particles, landing dust and sound, and a player radar (both can be turned off in Settings).
- 3DS: tap a player in the Players list to go next to them (not in races or the Dark Forest).
- 3DS: smooth day-night sky for games with a time of day.
- New Studio templates: Rising lava, Coin rush and Time trial.

## Installing
- Install `Blocksy.cia` with FBI, or copy `Blocksy.3dsx` to the `3ds` folder of the SD card and run it from the Homebrew Launcher.
