Blocksy is a multiplayer block-building game for the web and the Nintendo 3DS (homebrew), with shared worlds, chat and friends.

## 1.5.0, the final update (what's new since 1.4.1)

This is the last update of Blocksy. Thank you for playing, building and creating with us: your worlds, friends and achievements stay with you.

- Farewell: a thank-you screen on the 3DS (also in Settings > Thanks / Stats) with your play time, blocks placed, messages and games played, and a farewell message on the web (What is new and Credits).

### Changes
- Studio checklist with 3DS compatibility checks; fixed four untranslated texts.
- 3DS: friends show the game they are playing (X opens it); chat speech bubbles above players.
- 3DS: faces, backs, pets and trails in the Shop; pets and trails are drawn.
- 3DS: claim season levels and weekly missions from the Shop.
- 3DS: tree canopies in Hills and Islands are solid like on the web.
- 3DS: block debris particles, landing dust and sound, and a player radar (both can be turned off in Settings).
- 3DS: tap a player in the Players list to go next to them (not in races or the Dark Forest).
- 3DS: smooth day-night sky for games with a time of day; friends are highlighted in gold in the game and on the radar; you are lifted out of blocks placed on top of you.
- 3DS: 24 new game options in Settings, jump assist, hold X/Y to repeat, SELECT+X pick block, SELECT+Y undo, join/leave notices, colored FPS and ping.
- 3DS: in-game texts, login screens and login errors follow the language chosen in Settings (es/en/pt).
- New Studio templates: Rising lava, Coin rush, Time trial, Don't stop and Fireworks (a rocket show).
- Web: a syntax check test for every source file (a bad apostrophe broke the web build once).

## Installing
- Install `Blocksy.cia` with FBI, or copy `Blocksy.3dsx` to the `3ds` folder of the SD card and run it from the Homebrew Launcher.
