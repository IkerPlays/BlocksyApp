Devbuild 66: complete translations and 3DS sound effects
- Portuguese, French and German are now complete: the 713 texts that used to fall back to English (moderation, clans, meetups, the Studio, shop, privacy, settings, achievements and more) are translated. A new test checks that none is missing and that placeholders such as {n} are kept.
- The 3DS now has sound effects besides music: place and break blocks, jump, checkpoint, finish line and new chat messages. They are generated by the app (no files to download), play with the same DSP as the music (needs dspfirm.cdc) and can be turned off in Settings > Sound effects.
