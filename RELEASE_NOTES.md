Devbuild 62: reusable components and animation editor
- Studio Toolbox has a new My components section: save the selected parts (or the whole script) once and reuse them in any game. Click a component to place it (or append the script), right click to rename or delete. Export and Import move them between browsers or people as a .json file.
- New Animator panel at the bottom of the Studio. Part animations: keyframes with position, rotation, size, color and transparency, preview in the editor, and Apply writes a Lua script (TweenService) between BLOCKSY-ANIM marks in the game script. It needs a Lua script.
- Avatar emotes: up to 3 custom emotes per game (set the angle of legs, arms and head per keyframe). They appear in the emote wheel for everyone who plays that game. Web only.
- Components are stored in the browser of the person who saves them (not on the server).
