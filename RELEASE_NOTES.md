Devbuild 57: music on the 3DS
- The 3DS now plays the same playlist as the web (mp3 files from web/public/playlist). Music starts when you enter a game, shows the song title, and respects the song chosen by the game's creator.
- New settings on the 3DS: Music on or off and Music volume. It is on by default on New 3DS and off on the original 3DS.
- Needs dspfirm.cdc on the SD card (the app works without it, just silent).
- Instructions for adding songs are in web/public/playlist/README.txt.
