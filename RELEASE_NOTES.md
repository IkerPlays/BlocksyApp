Devbuild: new 3DS updater, Studio parts and skies on the 3DS, more templates
- New templates: Memory, Zones, Treasure hunt, Tower, Maze and Reflexes (all in 11 languages).
- 3DS: Studio parts can be translucent (water, glass) and Neon parts glow.
- 3DS: new setting to turn off automatic installation of stable updates.
- 3DS: music pauses while an update installs, freeing memory.
- 3DS updater: new "Test download (no install)" button that downloads and verifies the update without installing it, and a line with free RAM and SD space.
- 3DS: adaptive draw quality keeps the frame rate steady and draws more when there is spare time.
- 3DS: Home shows your next meetup (from the web calendar) next to the greeting.
- 3DS: distant hills on the horizon in worlds with a floor (two layers, tinted by the world type).
- 3DS: decorative flowers on grass worlds (visual only).
- Studio: four new structures (castle, dome, spiral staircase, pillar).
- 3DS: games use their sky setting (dawn, sunset, night, space): sky colors, fog, dimmer blocks, stars and moon.
- 3DS: new setting "Show FPS" prints the frame rate during games.
- New template: Crumbling floor (tiles vanish after you step on them).
- 3DS: the creator's welcome message is shown when you enter a game (like on the web).
- 3DS: downloads use bigger network chunks.
- 3DS: games with a time of day (config time) pick dawn, day, sunset or night; auto-cycle follows the console clock.
- Create game: filter templates by single player, multiplayer or build, and search them.
- 3DS: the game list is kept on the SD card and shown instantly on startup while the fresh list loads.
- New template: Quiz (step on the right answer; edit the questions in the script).
- 3DS: games now use their double jump, walk speed, gravity and spawn point settings.
- 3DS: fixed reading the game config when a game has Studio parts (world, sky and rules were cut off).
- Six new long-term achievements (Mason, Wrecker, Chatterbox, Regular, Legend, Tycoon).
- 3DS: games can set the fog distance (config fog).
- Studio warns when a game has more than 160 parts (the 3DS draws up to 160).
- Create game: "Surprise me" picks a random template.
- 3DS: Home shows your clan (tag, name and members) and your season level.
- 3DS: new setting "Show coordinates" prints your X Y Z position in games (handy for placing Studio parts).
- 3DS: Discover can be filtered by game mode with SELECT (all, build, social, obby, arena, adventure).
